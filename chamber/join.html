NP Membership Benefits

Designed for non-profit organizations in Santa Anita at no cost.

  • Directory listing under Non-Profit Organizations
  • Access to monthly community networking meetups
  • Invitations to public civic workshops

Bronze Membership Benefits

Ideal for small local businesses starting their chamber connection.

  • All NP Membership networking benefits
  • Standard business listing in the Chamber Directory
  • 10% discount on chamber seminars and workshops

Silver Membership Benefits

Built for growing businesses seeking stronger regional reach.

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Eligibility for Home Page spotlight rotation
  • 20% discount on business training and trade events

Gold Membership Benefits

Our highest tier for prominent visibility and leadership access.

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Priority spotlight placement on the Home Page
  • Complimentary passes to annual chamber galas and 30% event discounts
