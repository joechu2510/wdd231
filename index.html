About Me

My name is Joe Chu, and I am a 26-year-old creative professional and student from Peru. I am passionate about storytelling through media, and I work as a photographer and videographer while continuing my education. I have been studying online at Brigham Young University–Idaho since March 2026, and I am currently completing my final required course, WDD 231, to earn my first academic certificate.

I am married, and my wife and I share our home with two energetic 7-month-old puppies. Dedicated to service and personal growth, I served a full-time mission in the Taiwan Taipei Mission from 2019 to 2021. That experience broadened my perspective and allowed me to become fluent in Mandarin Chinese, which I speak alongside my native Spanish and English.
